Beneath the Surface

Quality Starts Beneath the Surface.

A durable floor, waterproof shower, or level tile surface is only as good as the foundational layers supporting it. Here is how we build surfaces that stand up to Massachusetts winters and daily family life.

LAYER 01

Reinforced Subfloor Structure

Joist reinforcement and screw-fastened plywood substrate with zero deflection.

LAYER 02

Moisture & Acoustic Membrane

Uncoupling and vapor barriers prevent subfloor cracking and sound transmission.

LAYER 03

Polymer-Modified Mortar / Fasteners

Commercial-grade adhesion bed ensures 95%+ coverage under every plank and tile.

LAYER 04

Finished Architectural Surface

Stain-resistant sealed grout, hardwood polyurethane, and expansion gap trim.

Cross-Section Engineering

Finished Tile / Hardwood Plank Top Layer
Polymer Mortar Bed & Grout Bond Layer
Uncoupling Vapor Membrane Protection
Solid Plywood Subfloor Bed Foundation

Engineered to prevent surface cracks, creaking boards, and moisture intrusion for decades.
